** Newsletter - July 2025
------------------------------------------------------------

** Amendment to the Decree comes into force
------------------------------------------------------------
On June 11, an amendment to the Decree came into force.

The new section 6.106 requires employers to provide the employees' date of birth with the first monthly report in which they are declared.

This obligation applies immediately to all new employees.

For employees who are already employed by you and whose dates of birth you may have failed to provide us with previously filed monthly reports, please note that you have until December 11, 2025, to provide us with the information.

You can provide us with the information using existing documents from your company or use the Date of Birth Form in the Employer section of our website: https://cpeep.qc.ca/en/employers/

For employers who use the portal to produce their monthly reports, you do not need to provide a form. Simply provide the date of birth when adding each new employee to the portal.
