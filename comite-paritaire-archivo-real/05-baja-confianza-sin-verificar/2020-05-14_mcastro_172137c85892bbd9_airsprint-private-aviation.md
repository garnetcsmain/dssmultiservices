Common Questions / Answers

  *   How does the Capital Purchase work?
     *   When you invest in Fractional ownership, you are buying time on one specific serial-numbered aircraft in 25-hour increments. The size of your share dictates how many guaranteed hours you can fly each year. As an AirSprint Owner, your time is guarantee on your type of aircraft anywhere in North America, Hawaii, the Caribbean and to/from Europe.
     *   As mentioned on the phone, if they’d like own the share corporately, as you do have title to a specific aircraft you’re able to use the depreciation on the share.
     *   The Capital purchase price is a one-time purchase price and is in USD. Jet globally are always bought and sold in USD.

  *   Exiting the program / selling your share
     *   At any point in time you can decide to sell your share.
     *   When you’d like to sell your share, we can provide guidance on the market value of your share, but you can set the price you’d like to get for the share.
     *   When we find a buyer for you, we will bring the offer to you, if accepted there is a 5% re-marketing fee for the sale. However, if you know someone who’d like to purchase your share we’d execute the transaction without the re-marketing fee.
     *   Over the last 2 years this process has taken us between 2-3 months to sell a share where the seller is looking for the retail value of the share.
     *   We have had individuals who have priced them more aggressively and they’ve cleared in as little as a week.

  *   How do the hours work?
     *   In each of your contract years you will have guaranteed access to your specific number of hours proportionate to the percentage of the aircraft you own.
     *   A whole aircraft is the equivalent to 800 hours of annual flying. We divided those 800 hours into 25-hour shares. You can purchase any number of hours you’d like in 25-hour increments. For example, a 75 -hour annual share is 9.375% of a whole aircraft. With that share size, you’d have access 365 days a year to fly 75-hours annually.

  *   What are the annual numbers?
     *   The Annual Management (overhead) fee is in Canadian dollars:
        *   This covers maintenance, landing fees, FBO fees, de-icing fees, insurance etc.
        *   There is a reduction in this fee as you become a larger Owner with AirSprint. A 50 hour share is 9% less than a 25 hour share and a 75 hour share is 17% less than a 25 hour share.
     *   Occupied Hourly rate
        *   This is the rate you pay while on-board the aircraft. You only pay for your time on-board and do not pay for the empty legs (or the return of the jet to the home base).
        *   There are no additional fees outside of the Occupied Hourly rate, plus the applicable fuel surcharge, for flying within Canada and the Continental US (the primary service area)

  *   History of the fuel surcharge
     *   The only number outside of the Occupied Hourly Rate is the fuel surcharge. I’ve attached some additional information (in the policy document) on how it works. In short, part of your Occupied Hourly Rate is allocated towards fuel. This sets the base fuel price at $1 CAD/ltr. If fuel for the previous month for domestic or international flights is above this value there is a fuel surcharge.
     *   Over the last 2 years the average domestic fuel surcharge as been $101 CAD/hr and international has been $651 CAD/hr for the Legacy 450s. It has been $70 CAD/hr domestically and $ 490 CAD/hr for the CJs.

  *   What about flying to Europe, the Caribbean and Mexico?
     *   You can do this on your jet. These are all part of our Secondary Service Area. There is a one-time fee associated with these flights, as they are outside of our Primary Service Area (Canada / Continual US).
     *   More info attached in the policies.

  *   How does the Lease work?
     *   Your access to the program is identical with the Lease vs. Capital.
     *   With the Lease it is a 5 year commitment and you’d have a refundable deposit and annual lease fee
     *   Number are in the attached PDF “2019 AirSprint Overview” on page 9.

  *   How are flights measured?
     *   All flights are measured as wheels up to wheels down plus a standard 6 minutes on each end of the flight for taxi / take-off. The times are rounded to the nearest 6 minute mark. For example, if your total time is 54 minutes, you’d be billed at 0.9 of an hour. If the aircraft is taxing longer than the 6 minutes, you’re not billed for the additional time.

  *   Empty Legs – Half price flights…
     *   With the Empty Leg flights, as an Owner, you use zero of your annual hours and the flights are at 50% off the Occupied Hourly Rate. The cost you see in the e-mailer is the total approximate cost.
     *   With the longer Empty Legs we can also be flexible on the departure / arrival airport. For example Toronto vs. Montreal on the “flexible” time empty legs.
