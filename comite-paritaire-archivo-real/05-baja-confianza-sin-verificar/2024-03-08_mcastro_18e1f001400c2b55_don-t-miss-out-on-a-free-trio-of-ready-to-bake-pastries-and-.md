March 8th, 2024

March brings the first maple harvest of 2024 and a sustainable sugar shack menu.

We have all the Quebecois classics from old-fashioned ham, beef tourtière from Nordest, homestyle bacon, and responsible sausages. Fear not vegans because Gusta’s maple and apple brunch sausage and Mi Corazon’s lentil-based creton is stocked up for the occasion. Pair these savoury plates with traditional baked beans from L’Héritage, organic split pea soup, multicoloured potatoes, and free-range eggs for a staple sugar shack meal. Add in some veg with beets, winter spinach, and apples for a killer crisp salad. And if there’s anything left in that 2024 can of organic maple syrup (yep, the trees have already been tapped), try our new sweet and salty coconut chips meal kit or boil it down for your very own tire sur la neige.

Free pastry bundle or organic blueb jam from our pastry pros.
This week on orders $100+ we’re gifting a trio of frozen pastries from Le Pain dans les Voiles. Try out a ready-to-bake buttery croissant, chocolatine, and raisin roulé to find your fave (which will most likely end in a tie!). As for our vegan Lufavores, get your butter knife ready because we’re gifting you a jar of their organic blueberry jam - a perfect addition to your collection of morning spreads.

Local pastries galore thanks to the Duvernay-Tardif fam.
Le Pain dans les Voiles has made strides with their four bakeries offering fresh breads, pastries, and organic fruit spreads crafted with local ingredients. Founded by Guylaine Duvernay and François Tardif, the duo has recently passed on the reins to their son Laurent (yep, the NFL champ). The family’s teamed up with farmers and millers in the Charlevoix and Montérégie regions to make their own flour. Beyond using it for the unreal lineup of frozen pastries available to Lufavores, they supply their homemade grain to 15 other bakeries around Montreal.

Warmer than usual weather brings early maple syrup bounty.
The sap’s already flowing but not for much longer if the temperatures stay in the positives, so try out the 2024 coulée while it’s fresh. Not to get sappy, but the unmatched crews at Ferme St-Ours, Érablière Varin, and Cabane à Boubou are supplying us with a bounty of organic syrups. They keep it responsible by not overtapping, using less water, and investing in energy-efficient electric machinery. Along with dark, maple, and amber varieties, our partners are also crafting  sugar taffy cones and barrel-aged syrups to hit all your sugary cravings.
