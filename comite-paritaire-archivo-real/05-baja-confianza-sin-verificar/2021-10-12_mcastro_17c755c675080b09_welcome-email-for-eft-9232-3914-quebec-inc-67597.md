Welcome to Cash Management and Thank You for selecting Web Business Banking (WBB) and our Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) Service.  With WBB, you will enjoy the speed and convenience of banking electronically, along with the ease of use of the internet.

  1.  Set Up Your Connect ID and Soft Token Authentication Device
You will receive your WBB Welcome Letter via email which will have your Connect ID and your Soft Token Authentication Device Serial Number. You will also receive a separate system generated email at your company email address from TD asking you to click on an embedded link to register your email address and create a password. The subject line of this email will read "You have a new TD Secure Email from: TD.WelcomeWBB@td.com/Vous<mailto:TD.WelcomeWBB@td.com/Vous> avez reçu un courriel sécurisé de la TD".

Once you've registered and created a password, you will receive a secure email that will contain a PDF attachment which will provide you with information about your Authentication Device(s) along with instructions on how to download the TD Token for Business app and use of the Authentication Device. You can open the PDF by using the password you created.

  1.  Book A Training Session
Please contact me to book a training session by Email (Reply to this email) after you have activated your Connect ID and temporary password.  Your Originator ID for the EFT Service will be set up in test mode and you will have a 60-day free period in which you can test the service.
