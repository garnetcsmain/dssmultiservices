DISALLOWED EXPENSES

To negotiate a reduction in disallowed expenses with the Canada Revenue Agency (hereinafter "CRA"), I need to know the nature of the expenses you claimed.

The CRA taxes a taxpayer on income. The smaller the income, the less tax the taxpayer pays. It is possible to reduce income by claiming expenses incurred to earn business or property income. In certain situations, it is also possible to deduct certain employment expenses against earned wages.

In your case, certain expenses were disallowed. This means that the CRA felt that these expenses did not meet the criteria required to be eligible to reduce QC Maintenance's income.

My goal is to convince the CRA that the disallowed expenses should have been accepted.

To do this, I need the following documents:

1.                  The 2015, 2016 and 2017 income tax returns (T2) of 9232-3914 Québec inc. ;

2.                  Income tax returns (T1) for you and Ms. Salazar, from 2015 to the present;

3.                  The exact expenses claimed to the CRA (I only have the total amounts);

4.                  Invoices or supporting documents for these expenses;

5.                  Proof of payment of these expenses (cheques, bank or credit statements, receipts);

6.                  Explanations for these expenses;

7.                  How do you and Mrs. Salazar pay yourselves, by salary or dividends?

For example, you claimed $44,575 in professional fees in 2017. I need invoices to prove that you spent this amount, and I need an explanation of why you required the services of a professional.

Attached is a document showing the value of expenses disallowed by the CRA.

UNEXPLAINED DEPOSITS

Francisco and Gloria

You explained that Francisco and Gloria are your parents and that they worked for QC Maintenance in exchange for credit card payments. You told me that you had not signed a contract with Francisco and Gloria.

To satisfy the CRA, you'll have to prove that these are not "gifts", but payments in exchange for work performed.

I therefore require the following documents:

1.                  Record of hours worked by Francisco and Gloria ;

2.                  Amounts collected from customers for Francisco and Gloria;

3.                  What was the nature of Francisco and Gloria's tasks?

4.                  An employee register from 2015 to 2017 ;

5.                  Francisco and Gloria's tax returns (T1) for the years 2015 to 2017 ;

6.                  Texts or emails in which you tell Francisco and Gloria what work needs to be done;

7.                  Francisco and Gloria's pay statements;

8.                  Proof of Employment Insurance and Canada Pension Plan payments for Francisco and Gloria;

9.                  Are Francisco and Gloria shareholders of QC Maintenance?

In Canada, employers are required to deduct Employment Insurance and Canada Pension Plan contributions from their employees' wages (s. 83 of the Employment Insurance Act and s. 21.1 of the Act to amend the Canada Pension Plan and the Old Age Security Act (pensions and benefits)), in addition to income tax deductions. Unless you have documentary proof that you have indeed made these withdrawals, it is possible that the CRA will reproach you and reclaim these amounts.
