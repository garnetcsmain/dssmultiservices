To be eligible to earn Aeroplan points ("Points") with Starbucks, you must:

   • Be an Aeroplan Program member and have an account in good standing, all in accordance with Aeroplan's Terms and Conditions;

   • Be a Canadian Starbucks Rewards member and have an account in good standing, all in accordance with the Starbucks Rewards Terms of Use; and

   • Link your Aeroplan Program account and Starbucks Rewards account by following the prompts available  at  aircanada.com/starbucks ("Account Linking"). You can only link one Starbucks Rewards account with one Aeroplan account. You can de-link your accounts at any time. However, if you choose to de-link your accounts, you will no longer be eligible to earn Points in accordance with these Aeroplan and Starbucks Promotion terms and conditions.

Once you've completed Account Linking, you can earn and redeem Points as described below.

1Up to 50 Aeroplan Points Offer: From March 6, 2023 12:00AM EST to March 12, 2023 11:59PM EDT, get 10 Aeroplan Bonus Points on every eligible transaction. This offer is valid for up to five times per Aeroplan member, to maximum of 50 Aeroplan points during offer period. Member must be an Aeroplan member in good standing, be a Canadian Starbucks Rewards member with an account in good standing. Member must be linked at time of transactions. To qualify, you must pay using your digital Starbucks Card or linked payment method in the Starbucks app, or by scanning your Starbucks app at the register prior to checkout. Please allow up to 6 weeks for points to be credited to your account.

2Starbucks Star Conversion: You can convert your Points into Stars at a rate of 400 Points for 200 Stars when you log into your Aeroplan account and visit your dashboard here and have a minimum Points balance of 400 Points. Once you convert your Points into Stars, you cannot reverse the conversion. Stars converted from Points are subject to the Starbucks Rewards terms of use. As a result, Stars converted from Points will expire six months after the calendar month in which such Stars were converted. For example, if you converted Stars on December 15, 2021, they will expire on July 1, 2022. The Stars in your Starbucks Rewards account are personal to you and may not be sold, transferred or assigned to, or shared with, family, friend or others, or used by you for any commercial purpose.

An “Eligible Transaction” is any purchase of food, beverage(s), and/or merchandise, excluding alcoholic beverages and Starbucks Card activations and reloads.

“Participating Starbucks Stores” are Starbucks locations in Canada that honor Starbucks Rewards. To locate stores that honor Starbucks Rewards, visit starbucks.ca/store-locator and look for stores that list “Redeem Rewards” as one of the store amenities.
